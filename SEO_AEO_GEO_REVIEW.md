**Technical delivery criteria:** A separate public landing page with one H1, crawlable text, self-canonical URL from its actual host, site-specific Open Graph and Twitter card tags, an accurate privacy policy, robots/index rules matching intent, and validated Organization/SoftwareApplication schema only if the facts are supported.
